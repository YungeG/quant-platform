## 1. Decision summary

The eight cases split into two materially different classes:

1. **One existing official filing missed by the provider capture:** `000046.SZ` published an audited FY2014 annual report on 2015-04-04 containing consolidated and parent-company balance sheets. Its absent S2A balance-sheet member is a source-capture/extraction gap, not an issuer non-filing.
2. **Seven official non-filings:** `000693.SZ`, `600090.SH`, `600146.SH`, `000038.SZ`, `000976.SZ`, `000622.SZ`, and `601028.SH` are covered by affirmative issuer, exchange, or regulator statements that the required annual report was not filed by the applicable deadline/screen. Their absent annual statements cannot be repaired by a broader search or by substituting drafts, estimates, quarterly reports, or later information.

For all eight issuers, the reviewed official chronology shows that the security was still legally listed at the relevant screen. Several were suspended, but suspension is a **trading-only status**, not termination of listing and not an S1 structural exclusion under the frozen contracts. This audit does not by itself establish complete S1 eligibility because accepted point-in-time authority is still required for every S1 limb: ordinary A-share identity, active listing, historical Main Board classification, official CSRC major category other than `J`, and fifth listing anniversary.

## 2. Classification rules used

- **VERIFIED official fact:** affirmatively stated in a primary issuer, exchange, CNINFO, or regulator document.
- **Source-bounded observation:** true only of the retained Tushare S2A capture or advisory probes; it is not completeness, non-filing, or historical-universe authority.
- **Unresolved absence:** a missing provider member for which competent authority has not established either an existing filing or an official non-filing. After this audit, none of the eight remains in that factual category; `000046.SZ` is an existing-filing capture gap and the other seven are verified non-filings.
- **S1 structural eligibility:** the deterministic five-limb test frozen in `quality-bband-staged-data-funnel-v1.md`. A later delisting, a current `list_status="D"`, an `ST`/`*ST`/`退` name, or a suspension cannot be backdated or treated as an S1 exclusion.
- **S2 missing-member closure:** authority that accounts for every expected issuer/period/API member without silently deleting it. An existing official filing must be ingested with statement lineage. Under the currently frozen missing-data policy, an official non-filing still produces an active S2 QB-DATA failure and no downstream manifest. Treating it as a counted terminal quality disposition would require an explicit user-approved contract change; it cannot become a fabricated statement row or an implicit threshold failure.
- **Trading-only status:** suspension or other inability to match orders. It may block execution under market rules, but it does not end broad-universe membership or prove structural ineligibility.

## 4. Cross-report reconciliation

### 4.1 What the S2A capture proves

The accepted S2A source snapshot proves retained-page integrity and that its terminal leaves contain 96,515 of 96,537 provisional expected API/issuer/period triples. It proves only that the 22 triples above are absent from that retained provider capture. The unretained endpoint probes and all current provider `list_status="D"` values remain **source-bounded observations** and supply neither historical S1 authority nor no-filing authority.

### 4.2 What the official audit adds

The primary sources remove the factual ambiguity behind all 22 absent triples:

- **1 triple (`000046.SZ`)** — an official filing exists and contains the missing statement; acquire and normalize it.
- **21 triples (the other seven issuers)** — competent official records affirm that the applicable annual report was not filed; no annual income statement, balance sheet, or cash-flow statement can be truthfully manufactured for the relevant point in time.

This is not permission to publish the current S2B design unchanged. The frozen funnel and missing-data policy say a required report/source/availability absence blocks S2 and emits no downstream manifest. Therefore S2B remains infeasible even though the cause of the provider absence is now resolved. A counted terminal non-filing disposition is a possible contract pivot, not current authority, and requires explicit user approval before a new packet is frozen.

### 4.3 S1 and trading status

The reviewed chronology supports the listing/age limb and rejects any attempted exclusion based solely on later termination or same-day suspension. It does **not** replace the competent S0/S1 closure required by the contracts. Before any accepted `structural_eligibility_manifest@1` can be published, an independently complete, point-in-time authority must exact-cover each screen for:

1. ordinary CNY A-share product identity;
2. listing interval containing the exact decision instant;
3. historical SSE/SZSE Main Board classification;
4. official CSRC major category not equal to `J`;
5. fifth calendar listing anniversary reached.

Suspension belongs in `trade_status_revision@1` and execution eligibility. It cannot be used to remove a listed security from broad membership or to rewrite the S1 predicate.

## 5. Required remediation packet

A resumable S2B packet needs all of the following, not another provider search:

1. **Accepted S1 manifest:** competent historical catalog/listing/product/board/CSRC-industry closure and one explicit S1 disposition for every broad-universe member at every screen.
2. **`000046.SZ` official-statement backfill:** immutable CNINFO bytes and deterministic extraction of the FY2014 consolidated balance sheet, including announcement availability, statement/presentation identity, currency, unit, revision lineage, required field mapping, and content hashes.
3. **Seven explicit official non-filing records:** issuer/period/report identity, deadline, first authoritative availability, source bytes/hash, and supersession lineage for each case.
4. **User decision on missing-member semantics:** either retain the current whole-stage QB-DATA failure, or explicitly approve a new public S2 rule that counts competent-source verified non-filings in exact cover and maps them to a named fail-closed terminal disposition. No issuer deletion, zero-row inference, forward-fill, estimate, draft, or implicit threshold-failure substitution is allowed.
5. **Rebuilt exact cover:** canonical Instrument identities, screen/period/union hashes, reconstructed S2A verification, extra-row accounting, the one official backfill, the seven non-filing dispositions, and a closure equation covering all 96,537 expected triples.
