# KORUUSDT factor universe and first research map

**Scope.** This report treats the duplicated user phrase “Korean equity index trend” as **one factor**, not two. It maps explanatory variables for research only; it is not a trading, leverage, or execution recommendation.

## Executive summary

Binance `KORUUSDT` is a 24/7, USDT-settled USDⓈ-M contract whose live exchange metadata identifies `contractType = TRADIFI_PERPETUAL`; its reference asset is the NYSE Arca-listed Direxion Daily MSCI South Korea Bull 3X ETF (`KORU`), not the KOSPI itself and not a crypto token. Binance launched it on **2026-06-22 at 13:55 UTC** with a 0.01 tick, 0.01 minimum quantity, 5 USDT minimum notional, eight-hour funding, 0% funding interest component, and ±2% funding cap; Binance expressly reserves the right to change several of those parameters, so live filters must be read from `/fapi/v1/exchangeInfo` rather than hard-coded ([Binance launch notice](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/88ea4a4f9f0b4ad4b7b308195c026fe4), [Binance filter definitions](https://developers.binance.com/legacy-docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/common-definition)).

The smallest defensible factor universe is: (1) the exact **MSCI Korea 25/50** benchmark, with KOSPI/KOSPI 200 used only as accessible broad-market alternatives or robustness controls; (2) **Samsung Electronics (KRX 005930)** and **SK hynix (KRX 000660)**; (3) **USD/KRW**, because Korean constituents trade in KRW while KORU and KORUUSDT are USD/USDT quoted; and (4) US-listed memory names **Micron (`MU`)** and **Sandisk (`SNDK`)**, plus **PHLX Semiconductor (`SOX`)** as a sector control rather than another memory claim. The first model should test lagged returns and session effects, not assume causality.

### Launch specifications versus time-sensitive specifications

At launch, Binance specified: **tick size 0.01 USDT**, **minimum trade amount 0.01 KORU units**, **minimum notional 5 USDT**, **funding every eight hours**, **funding cap/floor +2.00%/-2.00%**, and **0% interest-rate component** ([Binance launch notice](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/88ea4a4f9f0b4ad4b7b308195c026fe4)). Binance also says it may change funding, tick size, leverage, initial margin, and maintenance margin based on market risk. Therefore:

- Stable research metadata: symbol, perpetual contract type, USDT settlement, reference ETF, and original listing timestamp.
- Time-sensitive values: current tick, quantity step/minimum, minimum notional, funding interval/cap, leverage tiers, margin tiers, price-protection bounds, and contract status.
- Required live source before any empirical run: `GET https://fapi.binance.com/fapi/v1/exchangeInfo`, selecting `symbols[].symbol == "KORUUSDT"`. Read `PRICE_FILTER.tickSize`, `LOT_SIZE.minQty/stepSize`, `MARKET_LOT_SIZE`, and `MIN_NOTIONAL.notional`; Binance explicitly warns not to substitute display precision for tick or step size ([Binance filter definitions](https://developers.binance.com/legacy-docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/common-definition)).

This report records the exact published launch values but does **not** attest that mutable live filters remain unchanged.

### Index price, mark price, and funding

Binance exposes last price, index price, mark price, and funding separately. Mark price is the fair-value reference used for unrealized P&L and liquidation logic rather than the last traded price; the public market-data endpoint exposes `markPrice`, `indexPrice`, `lastFundingRate`, `interestRate`, and `nextFundingTime` ([mark-price API](https://developers.binance.com/docs/zh-CN/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Mark-Price), [liquidation/mark-price explanation](https://www.binance.info/en/support/faq/detail/360033525271)). Funding-rate history is available at `GET /fapi/v1/fundingRate`; Binance’s schema also distinguishes ordinary (`Regular`) funding from `Special` funding generated by stock dividends ([funding-history API](https://developers.binance.com/legacy-docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Get-Funding-Rate-History)).

For equity-linked TradFi perps, the underlying venue is not open 24/7. Binance describes a multi-mode arrangement: third-party reference data while the traditional market is open, with fixed/EWMA-style handling outside those hours; the KORUUSDT market carried a notice that it switched to EWMA mode on 2026-06-23 at 06:45 UTC ([Binance KORUUSDT notice](https://www.binance.com/en/futures/KORUUSDT?contentId=337114115017009), [Binance explanation of stock perpetual pricing](https://www.binance.com/ja/academy/articles/how-to-trade-stock-perpetual-contracts-on-binance)). The precise vendor basket and KORU-specific EWMA coefficients are not publicly identified in the retrieved first-party material, so they should not be invented.

### Corporate actions and split handling

KORU had a **20-for-1 forward split** effective July 2026. Binance suspended KORUUSDT for a contract-size adjustment beginning **2026-07-15 00:15 UTC**, completed the adjustment by **09:30 UTC**, allowed a five-minute cancel-only phase, and resumed continuous trading at **09:35 UTC**. Economically, the adjustment multiplied contract quantity/size by 20 and divided the reference price scale by 20 so aggregate exposure was intended to remain continuous ([Binance adjustment announcement](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/c226162366c54b78a7f98021b38e10c5), [Binance completion announcement](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/2ce887ba8fe14fdaa088e5bed7553a4e), [Direxion split announcement](https://www.direxion.com/press-release/direxion-to-split-nine-etfs)).

Research data must therefore use split-aware returns and must not interpret the approximately 95% raw price-scale drop as an economic loss. Preserve the adjustment window as an event flag; do not silently forward-fill across it.

### Current Platform compatibility boundary

The accepted Backtest Binance USD-M instrument model currently admits only metadata revisions whose `contract_type` is exactly `PERPETUAL`; any other value returns `UNSUPPORTED_CONTRACT_TYPE` (`backtest/packages/trading-kernel/src/crypto_quant_trading/profiles/binance_usdm/instrument_metadata.py`, `_failure_details`). Because live Binance metadata reports KORUUSDT as `TRADIFI_PERPETUAL`, the current Platform can collect and study the data but cannot yet claim an accepted Binance-profile backtest for this instrument. Supporting it requires a separately reviewed contract/profile extension; research code must not relabel it as `PERPETUAL` merely to pass admission.

## 2. What KORU actually represents

Direxion states that KORU seeks, **before fees and expenses, 300% of the performance of the MSCI Korea 25/50 Index for a single day**. It should not be expected to deliver three times the index’s cumulative return over periods longer than one day ([Direxion product page](https://www.direxion.com/product/daily-msci-south-korea-bull-3x-etf), [KORU fact sheet](https://www.direxion.com/uploads/KORU-Fact-Sheet.pdf)). It resets/rebalances daily, so multi-day results are path-dependent: volatility, compounding, financing, fees, derivatives tracking, and the sequence of returns matter.

The tracked **MSCI Korea 25/50 Index** measures Korean large- and mid-cap equities, covers approximately 85% of Korean free-float-adjusted market capitalization, and applies regulated-investment-company concentration constraints. MSCI’s methodology limits any group entity to 25%, and entities above 5% collectively to 50%, with buffers used at rebalance ([MSCI index page](https://www.msci.com/indexes/index/702747/msci-korea-25-50-index), [MSCI 25/50 methodology](https://www.msci.com/indexes/documents/methodology/2_MSCI_25_50_Indexes_Methodology_20241030.pdf)). The Direxion identifier for the benchmark is **Bloomberg index symbol `M1KR2550`** ([KORU fact sheet](https://www.direxion.com/uploads/KORU-Fact-Sheet.pdf)).

### Major exposure and the memory concentration

Direxion’s official page labels the following as **index top holdings** as of **2026-06-30**: SK hynix **27.41%**, Samsung Electronics **23.38%**, SK Square 4.30%, Samsung Electro-Mechanics 3.66%, Hyundai Motor 1.74%, KB Financial 1.58%, Samsung C&T 1.20%, Shinhan Financial 1.19%, Doosan Enerbility 1.11%, and Samsung Life 1.07% ([Direxion product page](https://www.direxion.com/product/daily-msci-south-korea-bull-3x-etf), [fact sheet](https://www.direxion.com/uploads/KORU-Fact-Sheet.pdf)). Samsung plus SK hynix therefore represented **50.79% of benchmark weight** on that date.

Important distinction: these are look-through **benchmark weights**, not necessarily line-by-line physical fund holdings. KORU commonly obtains exposure through ETFs, swaps, and futures and generally may not invest directly in Korean securities ([SEC summary prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424958/000119312526078549/d60837d497k.htm)). For factor research, Samsung and SK hynix are still essential because they dominate the benchmark’s economic exposure, but a holdings-reconciliation exercise should use Direxion’s dated daily-holdings CSV and derivative notionals rather than treating the index table as the fund’s custody ledger.

## 3. Minimal factor set

### Korean factors

1. **Exact broad benchmark: MSCI Korea 25/50 (`M1KR2550`).** This is the causal-mechanics anchor because it is KORU’s stated target. Use its official close/return series if licensed. MSCI provides index facts and methodology; historical licensed delivery may require a data agreement ([MSCI index page](https://www.msci.com/indexes/index/702747/msci-korea-25-50-index)).
2. **Accessible broad-market alternative: KOSPI or KOSPI 200.** Use only when the exact MSCI series is unavailable, or as a robustness control—not simultaneously as an unexplained duplicate broad factor. KRX is the first-party source for index OHLC and history ([KRX Data Marketplace](https://data.krx.co.kr/contents/MDC/MAIN/main/index.cmd?locale=en), [KRX KOSPI index family](https://index.krx.co.kr/contents/MKD/03/0304/03040200/MKD03040200.jsp)).
3. **Samsung Electronics common (`005930`, KRX/KOSPI).** It is one of the two dominant benchmark constituents. First-party prices: KRX Data Marketplace; official identifier corroborated by KRX filings ([KRX Data Marketplace](https://data.krx.co.kr/contents/MDC/MAIN/main/index.cmd?locale=en), [Samsung KRX filing](https://kind.krx.co.kr/common/disclsviewer.do?acptno=20260310001404&method=search)).
4. **SK hynix (`000660`, KRX/KOSPI).** It is the largest stated benchmark constituent in the dated official fact sheet. First-party prices: KRX Data Marketplace; identifier corroborated by KRX filings ([KRX Data Marketplace](https://data.krx.co.kr/contents/MDC/MAIN/main/index.cmd?locale=en), [SK hynix KRX filing](https://kind.krx.co.kr/common/disclsviewer.do?acptno=20260515002501&docno=&method=search&viewerhost=)).
5. **USD/KRW.** Include because Korean shares are priced in KRW while KORU is USD-priced and KORUUSDT is USDT-settled. A weaker KRW (higher KRW per USD) can reduce USD-translated Korean-equity returns, all else equal. Highest-trust accessible daily identifier: Federal Reserve H.10/FRED **`DEXKOUS`**, units KRW per USD; Bank of Korea ECOS is the domestic first-party alternative ([FRED DEXKOUS](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DEXKOUS), [BOK ECOS](https://ecos.bok.or.kr/)).

No Korean sector ETF, Hyundai, battery stock, China proxy, or macro-surprise series belongs in the first model: they add collinearity or narrative without being required by the stated factor universe.

### US memory/semiconductor factors

1. **Micron Technology (`MU`, Nasdaq).** This is the cleanest US-listed DRAM/NAND peer: Micron describes itself as delivering DRAM, NAND, and NOR memory and storage products ([Micron investor relations](https://investors.micron.com/overview/default.aspx), [Micron filing](https://investors.micron.com/static-files/c5f6c770-a182-417e-bb2b-6e192c403164)). Official/higher-trust prices: Nasdaq historical page, identifier `MU` ([Nasdaq MU historical](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/mu/historical)).
2. **Sandisk (`SNDK`, Nasdaq).** This supplies a distinct NAND/flash peer rather than another diversified compute company. Sandisk calls itself a NAND-flash data-storage developer/manufacturer and confirms Nasdaq ticker `SNDK`; its standalone listing began 2025-02-24, so its history is shorter ([Sandisk investor FAQ](https://investor.sandisk.com/ir-resources/investor-faqs), [Sandisk listing release](https://investor.sandisk.com/news-releases/news-release-details/sandisk-celebrates-nasdaq-listing-after-completing-separation), [Nasdaq SNDK historical](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/sndk/historical)).
3. **PHLX Semiconductor Sector Index (`SOX`; FRED `NASDAQSOX`) as a control.** SOX is not a memory-pure factor; it controls for broad US semiconductor risk so an apparent MU/SNDK relationship is not merely the whole chip sector moving. Nasdaq defines SOX as a modified-cap-weighted semiconductor index; official Nasdaq history is available, and FRED republishes the Nasdaq daily series under `NASDAQSOX` ([Nasdaq SOX overview](https://indexes.nasdaqomx.com/Index/Overview/SOX), [Nasdaq SOX history](https://indexes.nasdaq.com/Index/History/SOX), [FRED NASDAQSOX](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/NASDAQSOX)).

Nvidia, Broadcom, AMD, equipment makers, and foundries are deliberately excluded from the first model: they are important semiconductor companies but are not necessary to represent the user-specified **memory-related** factor once MU, SNDK, and SOX are present.

## 6. History and validation limitations

- Binance history starts **2026-06-22**, leaving only a short sample and few independent market regimes.
- The 2026-07-15 20-for-1 split/contract adjustment is a major structural break in raw prices and quantities.
- KORU history predates the Binance contract and is useful for constructing hypotheses, estimating session mappings, and checking daily-leverage behavior, but it cannot validate Binance-specific basis, liquidity, funding, mark-price mode, or overnight microstructure.
- Korean and US equity histories are longer still, but substituting them for KORUUSDT produces synthetic backtests that omit the perpetual’s index/mark design and 24/7 order book. Such tests are pre-listing hypothesis screens only.
- Current index weights, Direxion derivative exposures, and Binance filters change. Every dataset should store an `as_of` timestamp and source snapshot.
- With many lags and overlapping variables, false discovery is a primary risk. Keep the first specification small and reserve a true post-selection holdout.

## 7. Minimal recommended first research model

### Target and sampling

Run two complementary, deliberately small specifications:

1. **Session model (primary):** one observation per economically meaningful session boundary.
   - Targets: `r_koruusdt` from KRX close (06:30 UTC) to KORU US open; KORU US open-to-close; KORU close to next KRX open; and next-KRX-session return.
   - Use Binance **mark-price log returns** as the primary target and last-price returns as robustness; also model `basis = log(last/index)` separately.
2. **Intraday model (secondary):** 30-minute and 60-minute bars. Five-minute bars are permitted only for microstructure diagnostics because the external equity factors update on much slower venue clocks.

### Exact first-pass variables

All returns are log returns, split-adjusted, UTC timestamped:

- `r_msci_kr_2550` **or**, if unavailable, `r_kospi200` (choose one broad factor in the primary regression).
- `r_samsung_005930`.
- `r_skhynix_000660`.
- `r_usdkrw` from `DEXKOUS`, signed as log(KRW per USD).
- `r_mu`.
- `r_sndk`.
- `r_sox`.
- `r_koru_cash` when its cash session is open.
- Binance state variables: lagged `basis`, lagged funding rate, log volume, and binary indicators for KRX open, US core open, weekend/holiday, and the July 15 adjustment window.

Do **not** put `r_koru_cash`, the exact MSCI benchmark, KOSPI, Samsung, and SK hynix contemporaneously into every equation without checking collinearity. A minimal hierarchy is:

- Model A: broad Korea + USD/KRW + session flags.
- Model B: add Samsung and SK hynix; test whether they add information beyond broad Korea.
- Model C: add MU, SNDK, and SOX with only lags that are observable before the target interval.
- Model D: add lagged Binance basis/funding/volume.

Use ordinary least squares with heteroskedasticity/autocorrelation-robust errors for interpretability; compare against a historical-mean/null forecast. No nonlinear model is justified until the short live sample demonstrates stable incremental signal.

### Lags

- Korean variables: same KRX session close into later KORU/Binance windows; plus one KRX-session lag.
- US variables: same US session during overlap, and previous US close into the next KRX open.
- Binance variables: 1 bar and 1 session lag; weekend gap flag.
- Daily variables: lags 1 and 2 only. More lags would consume the already short live sample.

### Falsifiable hypotheses

- **H1 (broad Korea):** after a completed KRX session, broad Korean return has no incremental association with the subsequent KORUUSDT mark return after controlling for KORU cash return when available. Reject only with stable out-of-sample improvement.
- **H2 (memory concentration):** Samsung and SK hynix returns add no explanatory power beyond the broad Korean benchmark. Their official combined benchmark weight makes rejection plausible, but not assured.
- **H3 (FX translation):** USD/KRW adds no explanatory power after Korean local-currency equity returns. A positive `r_usdkrw` is expected to be adverse to USD-translated Korean exposure, but the sign must be estimated rather than imposed.
- **H4 (US memory lead):** lagged MU and SNDK returns add no information for the next KRX/KORUUSDT interval after controlling for SOX. This distinguishes memory-specific information from a broad chip beta.
- **H5 (closed-market basis):** KORUUSDT basis accumulated while KORU is closed contains no information about the next KORU opening gap. Test separately for ordinary weekdays and weekends.
- **H6 (stability):** coefficients and forecast improvements are unchanged across pre/post split-adjustment samples. Given the tiny pre-adjustment sample, failure to establish stability should be the default conclusion.

Use expanding-window or rolling-origin evaluation beginning only after a minimum estimation window; report out-of-sample error improvement, directional accuracy only as a secondary diagnostic, coefficient uncertainty, and results with/without the split window. The result is a research model, not a trade signal.

## Bottom line

The factor map should stay narrow: exact MSCI benchmark (or one KRX broad proxy), Samsung, SK hynix, USD/KRW, MU, SNDK, and SOX, with KORU cash and Binance basis/funding as instrument-mechanics variables. The central validation constraint is the recent Binance listing: longer ETF and equity histories can justify hypotheses, but only post-listing KORUUSDT mark/index/last/funding data can validate the final model.
