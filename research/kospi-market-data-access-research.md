# KOSPI Composite Index market-data access

> Archive note (2026-09-22; not a new research date): This preserves an earlier source brief. Products, prices, access/eligibility and rights below were not rechecked online for this commit; undated statements must not be treated as current offers or entitlements. URLs remain source locators, not proof of present availability. Before procurement or implementation, revalidate the exact first-party terms and obtain the required approvals. This archival task sends no inquiry, opens no account, acquires no data and grants no trading or redistribution rights.
>
> For the separate KIS Developers production/VTS support distinction, see the [dated primary audit](kis-apiportal-primary-audit.md); the eFriend terms cited below do not substitute for that API-specific review.

## Decision summary

Use **KRX/Koscom under a data-use contract** for a production real-time KOSPI Composite quote or any redisplay/redistribution. Use **KRX Data Marketplace / KRX OPEN API or the Korean public-data API** for authoritative end-of-day (EOD) history. Yahoo Finance is a practical **20-minute delayed, free-to-view** reference for `^KS11`, but not a production feed. Investing.com and TradingView are useful display/charting references, not evidenced here as licensed KOSPI APIs.

“Real-time” below means the provider represents the feed as real-time; it is **not** a promise of a particular millisecond latency. When a public source does not publish a price, delay, or KOSPI-specific entitlement, this note says so rather than inferring it.

## Practical selection

1. **Public EOD/backtests:** ingest the KRX OPEN API or Public Data Portal daily dataset; retain source timestamps and respect no-redistribution terms.
2. **Personal delayed dashboard:** Yahoo `^KS11` can be a labeled “20-minute delayed” display. Do not make order/execution decisions from it.
3. **Live internal trading:** use the executing broker only if its contract covers the intended account/workflow; otherwise Koscom/enterprise vendor.
4. **External product, alerts, or non-display algorithm:** begin with Koscom/ KR​X licensing or an enterprise-vendor order form. Specify KOSPI Composite, real-time vs delayed, display/non-display, user/device count, cache/history, derived data and redistribution territory. Do not start with a free web source and retrofit licensing later.

## Residual risks / verification before procurement

- KRX/Koscom’s public pages establish product types and policy but do not publish a price card or a numeric end-to-end latency; obtain a written quote and product specification.
- Bloomberg, LSEG and FactSet sources establish their delivery capabilities, not a proof that a prospective account is entitled to `KOSPI:IND`/the relevant RIC/FactSet symbol. Confirm the symbol, KRX index package, service level and rights with sales.
- TradingView’s KOSPI symbols are discoverable, but the reviewed evidence does not verify their KRX real-time status. Check the signed-in chart’s “Data is delayed” indication and Market Data page before claiming real-time.
- “KOSPI” can mean the Composite index, the KOSPI market, KOSPI 200, a sector index, or a Korea ETF. Put the exact instrument identifier and source timestamp in every downstream record.
